# Freight Forwarding

A freight forwarding job coordinates a shipment for a customer from start to delivery, by sea, air, road, rail or multimodal. From the job you can create the related import, export or transport request, so clearing and transport stay linked to the shipment.

## Overview

Go to **Clearing & Forwarding → Freight Forwarding**. Each job shows its FF number, logistics file, customer, **Mode**, **Direction**, ETD/ETA, route and status. Jobs are created from a logistics file with the service **Freight Forwarding - Local** or **Freight Forwarding - Transit**.

## Statuses

A job moves through **Draft**, **Booked**, **Scheduled**, **Picked Up**, **In Transit**, **At Hub**, **Customs Clearing**, **Transporting** and **Delivered**. Use the button at the top to advance to the next status, or **Jump to status** to skip ahead.

## Key Fields

- **Forwarding Type** (Local or Transit), **Mode of Transport** (Sea, Air, Road, Rail, Multimodal) and **Direction** (Import, Export, Cross-trade)
- **Incoterms**, **Booking Reference**, **MBL Number** and **HBL Number**
- **Parties**: shipper/exporter, consignee, notify party, shipping line or carrier
- **Route**: ports of loading and discharge, origin and destination country and city, vessel and voyage or flight number
- **Cargo**: description, cargo category, container count, weight and volume
- **Dates**: ETD, ETA, ATD, ATA, and pickup address, contact, date/time and instructions

## Linked Operations

Under **Operations**, select **Create Import**, **Create Export** or **Create Transport Request** to start the work that belongs to this shipment. They're listed under **Linked**.
