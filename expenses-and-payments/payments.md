# Payments

Record payments against approved expenses and keep track of what is still owed.

## Overview

Go to **Expenses & Payments → Payments**. The **Awaiting Payment** tab lists every approved expense that hasn't been fully paid. It shows the approved amount, how much has been paid, the balance and the status. The cards at the top show the number pending and the **Total Outstanding** in TZS. **All Payments** shows the payment history. Use the filter at the top right to switch between **Pending Payment**, **Partially Paid** and **Paid**.

![Payments awaiting payment](/images/import-walkthrough/payments-awaiting.jpg)

## Recording a Payment

1. Select the **+** icon on the expense's row.
2. Check the summary at the top: **Approved Amount**, **Already Paid** and **Remaining**.
3. Set the **Payment Date** and **Amount**. **Pay Full** fills in the remaining balance.
4. Choose the **Payment Method**. The **Payee** comes from the expense.
5. Add a **Reference** (bank reference, receipt number) and any **Notes**.
6. Select **Record Payment**.

![Record Payment dialog](/images/import-walkthrough/payment-record-dialog.jpg)

Kani LMS confirms with a payment number (for example *Payment PAY0006 recorded*). A fully paid expense leaves **Awaiting Payment** and shows **Paid** on its job. A part-payment leaves the row as **Partial** with the balance still showing, ready for the next payment.
