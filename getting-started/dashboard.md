# Dashboard Overview

After you sign in, Kani LMS opens on the **Dashboard**: a summary of how the business is doing over a chosen period.

![Dashboard](/images/getting-started/dashboard.jpg)

## Choosing the period

At the top, select **This Month** or **This Quarter**, or set your own **From** and **To** dates and select **Refresh**.

## Summary cards

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Revenue** | Invoiced revenue for the period |
| **Expenses** | Expenses for the period |
| **Net Profit** | Revenue less expenses |
| **Profit Margin** | Net profit as a percentage of revenue |
| **Pending Approvals** | How many requests are waiting for review or approval, and their total value |
| **Outstanding Receivables** | What customers still owe on approved invoices |

Revenue, expenses, net profit and margin also show the change against the previous period of the same length.

## Charts

- **Revenue, Expenses & Profit - Last 12 Months**: a month-by-month trend.
- **Profitability by Service**: revenue and expenses for each logistics file service (for example Transport Transit or Import Local) in the selected period.

## Layout

- **Sidebar (left)**: navigation grouped as Main (Dashboard, Logistics Files), Clearing and Forwarding, Fleet Management, Workshop, Expenses and Payments, Sales, Admin and Reports, and Integrations. You'll only see the items you have permission to access. See [Roles & Permissions](/administration/roles-and-permissions).
- **Top bar**: the sidebar toggle on the left, and on the right a **?** link to these docs, the light/dark theme toggle, notifications, and your profile menu.
- **Main content**: the page you've selected in the sidebar.

## Notifications

The bell icon shows a count of new notifications. Open it to see updates on your own requests, for example when an expense or fuel request you raised is reviewed, approved or rejected. Select **Mark all read** to clear the count.

## Collapsing the sidebar

Use the toggle at the top-left of the top bar to collapse the sidebar, which gives the content area more room on smaller screens.
