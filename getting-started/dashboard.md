# Dashboard Overview

After you sign in, Kani opens on the **Dashboard** - a snapshot of what's happening across your operations.

## Layout

- **Sidebar (left)** - navigation grouped by area: Clearing & Forwarding, Fleet Management, Expenses & Payments, Sales, Reports, Administration, and Integrations. You'll only see the sections and items you have permission to access - see [Roles & Permissions](/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).
- **Top bar** - search, theme toggle, notifications, and your profile menu.
- **Main content** - the page for whatever you've selected in the sidebar.

## Notifications

The bell icon in the top bar shows recent notifications relevant to you - for example, an approval waiting on you, or an update to a file you're following.

## Collapsing the sidebar

Use the toggle button at the top-left of the sidebar to collapse it to icons-only, which gives the content area more room on smaller screens.
